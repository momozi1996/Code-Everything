Create "Iridescence — Soap Bubbles". A soft, bright sky (#dbeafe → #fef3f2 gradient with faint drifting clouds). Bubbles of varied sizes float upward and drift on a gentle wind, wobbling as their shape oscillates; each is rendered with thin-film interference colours — swirling bands of magenta, gold, cyan and green on a mostly transparent sphere — plus a bright window-shaped specular highlight and a smaller secondary one, with the swirl animating over time (canvas gradients and noise-driven hue shifts).
