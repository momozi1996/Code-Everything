## Prompt

```
Create "Somewhere Inside", a double-exposure art piece: a large human profile silhouette (a smooth SVG path of a face in profile with shoulders) acts as a mask revealing a whole landscape inside it — layered mountain ranges, a pine forest, a lake reflecting the moon, drifting fog and birds — while outside the silhouette is a pale misty gradient. Palette: a dusk gradient inside (#1d2b53 → #7e2553 → #ff9e7d), mist outside (#f2efe9 → #d9d4cc), ink details. The inner landscape moves in subtle parallax with the pointer (and slowly on its own), fog layers drift, and the moon's reflection shimmers. Original poetic text fragments fade in and out beside the profile in an elegant italic serif. Interaction: pointer parallax; clicking cycles the inner landscape between "Mountains", "Ocean" (waves and a lighthouse) and "City" (skyline with lights), each with its own palette and a smooth cross-fade. A film-grain overlay. The composition reflows for mobile, with the silhouette scaled and centred.
```
