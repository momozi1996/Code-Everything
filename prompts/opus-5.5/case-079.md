# 三张塔罗牌互动

```
Create "Arcana Nocturne — A Three-Card Reading". A midnight-purple ground (#140a24) with drifting star dust and a velvet tablecloth texture; gold-foil (#d4af37 → #f7e7a1) and moonlight-silver accents. A deck of the 22 Major Arcana, each face designed procedurally in SVG with an original geometric/esoteric motif (e.g. The Moon: crescents and a path between two towers; The Star: eight-pointed stars above water; The Tower: lightning-struck tower geometry), framed with an ornate gold border, a Roman numeral and the title in engraved serif capitals. The back: an intricate symmetric pattern around a central sun/eye. Interaction: "Shuffle" animates the deck riffling; the user fans the deck and picks three cards (Past, Present, Future), which fly to their positions and flip in 3D with a glint; each revealed card shows an evocative, original short interpretation (framed as entertainment). Cards tilt with a shimmer on hover. Optional reversed cards. Typography: serif small caps with generous letter-spacing; body text in an italic serif.
```
