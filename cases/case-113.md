## Prompt

```
Create "Pages from Juno's Sketchbook" (a fictional illustrator), a portfolio designed as a physical sketchbook. Dot-grid paper (#fbf8f1 with faint blue dots), graphite lines (#3d3d3d), highlighter yellow (#fff176) and a single red-pencil accent (#d64545). All illustrations are SVG line drawings with a hand-drawn wobble (SVG turbulence displacement plus slightly irregular paths) that draw themselves as they enter the viewport. Elements: washi-tape strips holding polaroid-style frames of CSS/SVG doodle artworks; sticky notes in a casual handwriting stack ("Segoe Print", "Bradley Hand", "Comic Neue", Chalkboard, cursive); margin doodles (coffee rings, arrows, stars); a spiral binding along the left edge. Sections: a hello note, selected work (six sketched projects), process scribbles, and contact written on an envelope. Interaction: pages flip horizontally with a page-curl animation on desktop and scroll on mobile; doodles wiggle on hover; a pencil cursor leaves a faint fading trail.
```
