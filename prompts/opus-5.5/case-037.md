# 程序化植物标本柜

```
Design "Herbarium Imaginarium", a Victorian botanical specimen cabinet of procedurally grown plants that do not exist. Aged paper (#efe6d2 with foxing spots and fibre texture from layered radial gradients), sepia ink (#4a3622), faded botanical greens (#6b7f4e, #9aa77a) and madder-red accents for seeds and berries. Each specimen card shows a plant grown by an L-system/recursive branching algorithm and drawn in SVG with ink-like stroke variation, leaves, buds and occasional flowers; a paper tape strip "holding" the stem; a handwritten-feeling label in an italic serif with an invented Latin binomial (generated from syllable lists), a collection date and a specimen number. A grid of six specimens on desktop, one or two on mobile. Interaction: "Collect a new specimen" grows a plant stroke by stroke (animated stroke-dashoffset); clicking a card opens it large in a lightbox with a magnifying-loupe hover; a "Press" button toggles a flattened pressed-flower look (desaturated, slightly translucent). Typography: "Iowan Old Style", Palatino, "P052", Georgia, serif, with italic labels and ornamental rules.
```
