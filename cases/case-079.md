Create "Arcana Nocturne — A Three-Card Reading". A midnight-purple ground (#140a24) with drifting star dust and a velvet tablecloth texture; gold-foil (#d4af37 → #f7e7a1) and moonlight-silver accents. A deck of the 22 Major Arcana, each face designed procedurally in SVG with an original geometric/esoteric motif (e.g. The Moon: crescents and a path between two towers; The Star: eight-pointed stars above water; The Tower: lightning-struck tower geometry), framed with an ornate gold border, a Roman numeral and the title in engraved serif capitals.
