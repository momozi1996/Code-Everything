## Prompt

```
Build "Horologe — The Word Clock", a typographic clock that spells the current time in words on a grid of letters (an original English layout in the letter-grid clock tradition: "IT IS HALF PAST TEN", five-minute precision plus four corner dots for single minutes). An 11×10 letter grid in a clean, wide-tracked sans; inactive letters are faint (#2a2a2a on #111) and active ones glow warm white (#fff6e5) with soft bloom, fading on and off in a gentle stagger when the time changes. Faces: "Obsidian" (dark), "Paper" (ink on #f3efe6), "Copper" (letters etched into a brushed-copper gradient, lit ones glowing) and "Neon" (magenta tube glow). A small subtitle gives the date in words. Interaction: a face switcher; clicking the grid toggles between the English layout and a second-language layout (e.g. Spanish); a fullscreen button; and a "demo" button that runs through a whole day in 20 seconds. The clock scales to fill any screen while keeping its square grid.
```
