# 建筑与景观工作室

```
Design "MONOLITH — Architecture & Landscape" (a fictional studio), a brutalist-minimal architecture portfolio: raw concrete surfaces (procedural texture from layered noise with formwork tie-hole dots and board-mark grain) in greys #8d8d8a, #b5b3ad, #d6d4ce and charcoal #262624, with a single accent of oxidised-copper green #4f7f73. Typography: huge, tight, uppercase grotesk headlines and precise small captions with project data (location, year, area in m²). Composition: a severe grid, vast negative space, heavy slabs. Five fictional buildings appear as axonometric SVG drawings of monolithic volumes whose light and shadow shift as a "sun angle" slider moves (shadows computed from the angle). Scroll storytelling: slabs slide in; a building assembles piece by piece as you scroll; a horizontal material-library strip shows concrete, corten, timber and glass as procedural textures. Interaction: the sun slider; hovering a project rotates its axonometric slightly; clicking opens a project sheet. Calm, heavy, precise motion.
```
