## Prompt

```
Create "Meridian Metro", an animated transit map for a fictional city in the tradition of Beck and Vignelli diagrams. White ground (#fbfbf9); six coloured lines (#e4002b red, #0072ce blue, #00a651 green, #ffb81c amber, #9b26b6 purple, #00b2a9 teal) drawn as thick SVG paths with only 45° and 90° bends; interchange stations as white capsules with black outlines, regular stations as tick marks; a river as a pale blue band and a park area. Station names in a clean humanist sans with careful placement. Trains (small rounded rectangles in the line colour) run along the lines on simulated schedules, pausing at stations. Interaction: hover a line to spotlight it (others fade); hover or click a station for a card with next departures whose countdowns tick live; a route planner — pick origin and destination to highlight the shortest path (Dijkstra on the station graph) with transfer count and estimated time; zoom/pan on mobile. The line legend doubles as toggles. Include a subtle "service status" panel ("Good service", "Minor delays"). It should look like a crisp printed map.
```
