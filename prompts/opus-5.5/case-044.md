# 层叠剪纸森林

```
Design "Foxglove Hollow", a layered paper-cut forest diorama. Seven to nine stacked layers of hand-cut-looking paper (SVG paths with slightly irregular edges) — distant hills, pines, a winding river, mushrooms, ferns, a small fox and foreground grasses — each casting a soft drop shadow onto the layer behind it so the scene has real depth. Day palette: sage #9bb89b, moss #5f7f5b, deep teal #274e52, ochre #d9a45b, burnt orange #c8642d, cream sky #f3e8d2. Night palette: indigo layers, tiny glowing windows, fireflies and a paper moon. Subtle paper grain from procedural noise. Interaction: pointer movement creates parallax between layers (touch-drag on mobile); a day/night toggle recolours every layer with a staggered transition; clicking the fox makes it hop to a new spot; paper leaves drift gently. The title sits on a small paper tag hanging from a string, in a warm serif. The composition must recompute nicely for portrait screens.
```
