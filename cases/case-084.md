## Prompt

```
Design "RADICAL SHAPES — Milano Design Fest '86" (fictional), a Memphis-Group-inspired event landing page. Palette: black #111, pink #ff90b3, turquoise #2ec4b6, yellow #ffd23f, lilac #b388eb, red #ef476f on white, with terrazzo/confetti patterns and squiggle backgrounds (SVG patterns). The composition is bold, playful and asymmetric: geometric objects (cylinders, zigzags, dotted triangles, half-moons) float and rotate slowly around the hero title. Typography: chunky geometric sans headlines, bold outlines and two-colour offset text shadows. Sections: a countdown to the fictional opening with flip digits; a lineup of fictional designers on cards with patterned headers; a schedule timeline; a venue map made of playful illustrated blocks; and ticket tiers. Interaction: shapes bounce away from the cursor; clicking the hero spawns new random Memphis shapes with a pop; cards wobble on hover; a pattern-shuffler button changes every background pattern. Keep chaos from overwhelming readability: a strong grid underneath.
```
