# 审计 AI 业务工作流

```
I want a fresh-eyes audit of how my business runs with AI. Look at everything you can access: my files, notes, projects, workflows, goals, and past conversations.

My goal: I want you to be able to plan, build, run, track, improve, and learn from the work on every project I have, with way less steering from me. Right now it's too clunky, and I'm the one leading every step.

Here's what I want back:

1. What you see. A quick summary of how I work today, in plain English.
2. Where I'm the bottleneck. Every spot where the work stops until I step in.
3. A scorecard. Rate each stage from 1 to 10 (plan, build, run, track, improve, self-improve) with one line on why.
4. The top 5 fixes, ranked by how much of my time they give back. For each one: what to change, what it unlocks, and the first step.
5. What's missing. The systems, structure, or info you'd need to run more of this on your own.
6. Quick wins. Anything we can fix today in under 30 minutes.

Rules:
- Be blunt. I want the real read.
- Use plain language. No jargon.
- Don't invent anything. If you can't see it, say so.
- Make smart assumptions and flag them. Don't interview me first.
```
