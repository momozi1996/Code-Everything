## Prompt

```
Create "Crystalline — No Two Alike", a procedural snowflake generator. Each snowflake grows with 6-fold (dihedral) symmetry using a branching algorithm — main arms with side dendrites, plates and needles that vary with "temperature" and "humidity" parameters, loosely echoing the Nakaya diagram. Rendered in SVG/canvas as translucent ice with inner facets, edge highlights and a faint glow on a deep winter-blue background (#0c1b33 → #1c3a5e). The hero shows one large flake slowly rotating; below it, a gallery of smaller generated flakes. Background: gentle snowfall of micro-flakes with depth blur. Interaction: "Grow a snowflake" animates growth outward from a hexagonal seed; temperature and humidity sliders show how the form changes (plates at some settings, dendrites at others — keep the explanation light and approximate); clicking a gallery flake enlarges it; each flake gets a catalogue number and date; download as SVG. Typography: a thin, wide-tracked sans with a delicate serif italic.
```
