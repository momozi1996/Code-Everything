```
Create "MYTHIC — Holographic Card", a premium collectible trading card with realistic holographic foil. One large card centred on a dark velvet background (#0e0b16 with a soft spotlight). The foil: layered conic/linear rainbow gradients with color-dodge/overlay blend modes, a procedural glitter texture, and a diagonal glare band — all driven by pointer position so the rainbow shifts and the glare slides as the card tilts in 3D (rotateX/Y with spring easing and perspective). An embossed frame, a stat block (ATK/DEF, rarity stars), italic-serif flavour text and a set number "077/250". Interaction: pointer tilt (touch-drag on mobile); click to flip the card to its patterned back; a "Pack opening" button plays a short sequence in which a foil pack tears open and the card slides out with a flash. Foil palette: iridescent pastels over deep indigo art.
```
