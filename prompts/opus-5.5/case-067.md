# 雨夜窗上的水滴

```
Create "Midnight Window", rain on a window pane at night. Behind the glass, a canvas shows a blurred city bokeh scene (soft glowing circles in amber #ffb347, warm white, tail-light red #ff4b4b and teal signage #3fd0c9 on deep blue-black #0a0f1f), drifting slowly. On the glass: realistic raindrops — small static beads plus larger drops that grow, then slide down in wobbly paths leaving trails and merging with others; each drop refracts the background (render the background flipped and magnified inside the drop). Condensation fogs the glass; the user can wipe it clear with pointer or finger, and the cleared area slowly fogs back. Ambient layers: occasional distant lightning and rain sound synthesized with Web Audio (filtered noise plus droplet ticks) behind a "sound" toggle. A small caption in a light serif: "It's 00:47. The city is still awake." using the live local time. Controls: rain intensity slider and a city/forest background toggle (forest = green bokeh). Performance-minded.
```
