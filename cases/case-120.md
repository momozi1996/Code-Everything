## Prompt

```
Build "Aquarelle — Generative Watercolor Landscapes". Each generation paints a new landscape in convincing watercolour: layered translucent washes built by recursively deforming polygons (many low-alpha layers with jittered edges) for sky gradients, distant hills, mid-ground mountains, a lake, trees as dabbed blobs, and a sun or moon — all on textured cold-press paper (procedural grain, with pigment darkening where it pools at edges). Palettes rotate among seasonal watercolour sets: "Ultramarine & Burnt Sienna", "Sap Green Spring", "Quinacridone Sunset" and "Payne's Grey Winter". The painting builds up layer by layer over about six seconds, as if being painted. Interaction: "Paint another"; palette choice; a "wet-in-wet" slider controlling bleed; clicking the paper drops water that creates a cauliflower bloom in the wet wash; "Sign & download" adds a small signature and date before saving a PNG. The painting sits on a wooden easel/table background. Typography: a delicate serif with a script-like italic.
```
