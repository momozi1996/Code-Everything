## Prompt

```
Build "QUANTA Terminal", a dark, premium trading dashboard with clearly fictional tickers and simulated data (labelled "Simulated market"). Near-black ground (#0b0e11), panel surfaces #12161c, gains emerald #16c784, losses crimson #ea3943, gold accent #f0b90b, hairline borders. Layout: a scrolling ticker tape at the top; a main candlestick chart (canvas) with volume bars, moving averages, a crosshair with OHLC tooltip, time-range tabs and smooth panning/zooming; a live order book with depth bars that flicker as orders update; a recent-trades feed; a watchlist with sparklines; a portfolio allocation donut; and a buy/sell ticket with validation and a confirmation toast. Data evolves in real time via a random walk with volatility clustering. Typography: tabular numerals, a clean sans, monospace for prices; prices flash green/red on change. Responsive: panels reflow into tabs on mobile. Smooth and crisp at high DPI.
```
