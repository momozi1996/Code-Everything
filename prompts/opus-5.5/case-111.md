# 新艺术风格茶馆

```
Design "Maison Lierre — Botanical Teas, Paris 1899" (a fictional tea house) as an Art Nouveau poster page in the spirit of the era's great poster artists (original artwork, nothing copied). Palette: muted sage #a3b18a, olive #588157, antique gold #c9a227, terracotta #bc6c25, cream #fefae0, deep teal #264653. The page is framed by an ornate SVG border of whiplash curves, stylised lilies and ivy that draw themselves on load and sway gently; a circular halo motif with radiating mosaic sits behind the title; flowing, organic panels hold the content (the house's story, three signature blends, an afternoon-tea menu, a contact card). Decorative initials and dividers. Typography: a serif stack plus custom SVG-drawn title lettering with curved terminals. Interaction: hovering a blend grows vines around its card; a subtle gold sparkle on the halo; a toggle between "Day" (cream) and "Salon" (deep teal with glowing gold lines). Graceful responsiveness: the border simplifies on phones.
```
