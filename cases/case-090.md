## Prompt

```
Build "Fridge Verse — Magnetic Poetry", a tactile word-magnet toy. A large enamel fridge door (subtle vertical gradient #eef0f2 with faint metallic noise and a chrome handle at the side) holding about 120 word magnets: small white tiles (#fffdf7) with a slight drop shadow and a serif word, each rotated slightly at random, a few coloured (pale pink, mint, butter) for flair. Choose a vocabulary (nouns, verbs, adjectives, suffixes -s, -ing, -ed) that makes poetic combinations likely. Dragging a magnet gives a pleasing lift (shadow grows, slight rotation) and a snap back onto the door; magnets may overlap. Features: a "Shake" button that scatters magnets with physics; theme packs (Love, Ocean, Space, Kitchen); a poem detector that reads tiles arranged in lines left to right and shows the current poem in a small card you can copy; a few decorative fridge items (a drawn polaroid picture, a fruit magnet). Persist positions in localStorage. Typography: classic serif tiles with a rounded sans for the UI.
```
