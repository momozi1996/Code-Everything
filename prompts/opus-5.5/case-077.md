# 冬夜木屋交互场景

```
Create "Hytte — A Winter Cabin", a cosy interactive scene: the interior of a log cabin at night, illustrated in SVG/CSS with warm wood tones (#7a4a2a, #a0673c). A stone fireplace with an animated fire (layered flame shapes with flicker plus canvas embers rising); a large window onto a snowy spruce forest with falling snow at several depths and a green-violet aurora (#4dffb4 → #8a5cff) rippling in the sky; a steaming mug on the sill; a reading lamp; and a sleeping cat that breathes. Warm vs cold light: the firelight flicker subtly modulates the room's lighting. Interaction: click the lamp to toggle it (room lighting changes); click the fireplace to add a log (the fire flares, more embers); click the window to open it (cold wind — snow blows inside briefly and the fire dims); click the cat to make its ear twitch; an optional ambient sound toggle (synthesized fire crackle and wind). A title placard in a Scandinavian-style serif. The composition adapts to portrait screens (window above, fireplace below).
```
