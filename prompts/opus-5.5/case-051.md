# 等距迷你城市建造

```
Create "Pocket Metropolis", an isometric tiny-city builder toy. A canvas-rendered isometric grid (12×12 on desktop, fitted on mobile) in soft pastels: grass #a8d5a2, water #7ec8e3, roads #d6d0c4, and buildings in peach #ffb5a7, blush #fcd5ce, mint #b8e0d2 and lavender #cdb4db, with gentle ambient-occlusion shading on each block face. A bottom toolbar places houses, towers, trees, parks, roads and water; buildings pop in with a squash-and-stretch bounce. Little cars travel along connected road tiles and tiny people stroll in parks. A day/night cycle slowly shifts the lighting (sky gradient behind, windows lighting up at night, glowing streetlamps). A stats bubble shows population and happiness (parks near homes increase it). Interaction: click/tap tiles to place; right-click/long-press to bulldoze with a puff of dust; drag to pan; pinch/scroll to zoom; "Random town" seeds a charming city. Persist to localStorage. Typography: a rounded, friendly sans.
```
