The pointer is a gentle lantern: fireflies drift toward it when it is held still and scatter when it moves fast. A crescent moon rises slowly and stars appear as the sky darkens. Minimal controls at the bottom-left that fade when idle: sync strength, wind, and sound (synthesized crickets). Title in a delicate italic serif.
