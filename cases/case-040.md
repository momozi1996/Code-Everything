## Prompt

```
Design "MOVE — A Manifesto in Motion", a scroll-driven kinetic typography experience. Black background (#0a0a0a), off-white type (#f1f1ec) and one acid accent (#c6ff00). Seven original manifesto statements about motion, attention and design each get a full-screen scene with a distinct typographic motion driven by scroll progress: letters assembling from scattered positions; words stretching horizontally with scaleX; a line splitting in half to reveal an accent line; characters cascading like dominoes; a word rotating in 3D; a sentence typed out with a caret; and a giant word zooming until its counter becomes the next scene's background. Use sticky sections and custom scroll-progress mapping, with CSS transforms only for performance. Typography: a heavy grotesque/condensed sans stack for display and monospace for small annotations such as scene numbers ("SC. 03 / 07"). A progress rail on the side. Reduced motion: statements appear statically with simple fades.
```
