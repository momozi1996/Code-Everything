# 赛博朋克雨夜街景

```
Create an immersive cyberpunk street at night titled "NEON RAIN — District 9 Night Market" (fictional). A full-viewport canvas scene with four parallax layers of skyline silhouettes (#05010f → #1a0433) and randomly lit windows; holographic vertical signs with katakana-like glyphs drawn in canvas that flicker with believable neon failure (brief dropouts, buzz); a wet street reflecting the signs (mirrored, blurred, rippled); dense diagonal rain with splash particles on the ground. Palette: hot magenta #ff2a6d, cyan #05d9e8 and acid-yellow #f9f002 accents on ink black. Overlay a thin HUD: time, temperature, "SIGNAL" bars and a scrolling ticker of fictional news in monospace. Typography: condensed uppercase sans with chromatic-aberration text-shadow. Interaction: the pointer moves the parallax camera; a click triggers a lightning flash that briefly lights the whole skyline plus a thunder rumble (Web Audio noise, after a gesture, with a mute toggle); a toggle switches rain intensity. Must stay fluid at 60fps using pooled particles.
```
