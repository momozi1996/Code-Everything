## Prompt

```
Build me a top-down zombie survival game I can play in the browser: I move with WASD, aim with the mouse and click to shoot, zombies come from the edges and chase me, they come in waves that get bigger, I have health and a score, and it shows game over with a restart button. Make it look good, not like a prototype.

People who ask for a game expect a BEAUTIFUL game, not blocky primitive geometry. Do not default to cubes/cylinders/capsules for anything alive.
```
