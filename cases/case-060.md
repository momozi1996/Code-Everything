## Prompt

```
Create "Currents — Flow Field Studio", generative art made of thousands of particles following a Perlin/simplex noise vector field (implement the noise inline). Particles leave long, fine, semi-transparent strokes that accumulate into silky flowing textures like wind maps or marbled paper. Five curated palettes, each with its own background: "Kyoto Dusk" (#1a1423; #e8a87c #d45d79 #6e5773 #ea9085), "Glacier" (#f4f7f6; #264653 #2a9d8f #8ab17d), "Ember" (#0d0d0d; #ff4800 #ff7b00 #ffb700), "Riso" (paper #f2eee3; fluorescent pink #ff48b0 and blue #0078bf with multiply blending) and "Mono Ink". A slim, elegant side panel holds noise scale, particle count, stroke length, curl/turbulence, palette, a seed field, "Regenerate" and "Download PNG". Clicking the canvas plants an attractor/vortex that bends the field locally; the piece slowly evolves over time (the noise's z-axis). Typography: understated monospace labels and a serif title. Must stay performant (typed arrays, batched paths).
```
