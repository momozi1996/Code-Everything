## Prompt

```
Design "ATELIER NOIRE — Autumn/Winter Collection" (fictional), a high-fashion editorial lookbook. Palette: bone #efe9e1, blush #e8c4b8, oxblood #5a1a1d, black #0d0d0d. Typography is the star: an ultra-high-contrast Didone display stack (Didot, "Bodoni 72", "Bodoni MT", "C059", serif) set enormous and overlapping the imagery, mixed with tiny tracked sans labels. Since no photographs are allowed, each "look" is an abstract fashion illustration built from CSS/SVG — elongated silhouettes, draped fabric shapes with gradient folds, a hat brim, the geometry of a coat — elegant and minimal like fashion croquis. Layout: a horizontal-scroll gallery on desktop (the wheel drives horizontal movement) and vertical on mobile; asymmetric compositions with numbered looks ("LOOK 07"), material captions, and a giant word ("RESTRAINT") sliding in parallax behind. Interaction: hovering a look reveals a detail-zoom fabric swatch (procedural tweed, silk sheen, herringbone); a custom cursor becomes a thin ring labelled "VIEW"; a progress bar. Page-load intro: a curtain wipe.
```
