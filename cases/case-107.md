## Prompt

```
Design "SIGNAL/NOISE", the portfolio of a fictional glitch artist, "k.oshiro". Stark black #000 and white #fff with RGB channel accents (#ff0040, #00ff9f, #0070ff). The page is intentionally corrupted but controlled: a headline with RGB split and horizontal slice displacement that re-randomises periodically; project thumbnails that are generative canvases (pixel-sorted gradients, datamosh-like block smears, scanline interference) and glitch harder on hover; CRT scanlines and occasional full-screen tear events; a scrolling "corrupted" hex-dump sidebar. Sections: a works grid (eight generative pieces with titles and years), an about text in monospace that types itself with random character corruption resolving into readable text, an exhibitions list and contact. Interaction: hovering intensifies the glitch; clicking a work opens it full-screen with a "corruption" slider; a "stabilize" toggle calms every effect (and prefers-reduced-motion defaults to stable). Typography: monospace and a heavy grotesk. Despite the chaos, it stays navigable and legible.
```
