## Prompt

```
Create "Selene — Tide & Moon Clock", a poetic instrument showing today's moon phase and a stylised tidal cycle. Compute the current moon phase from the date (synodic month ≈ 29.53059 days from the reference new moon of 2000-01-06 18:14 UTC) and render a large, photoreal-leaning moon on canvas with a correct terminator, craters and maria from procedural noise shading, and earthshine on the dark side. Below it, a flowing sea of layered animated SVG waves (navy #0b1d3a, #13315c, #1d4e89 with silver highlights #c9d6e3) whose level rises and falls with a simplified semi-diurnal tide model (two highs and two lows per ~24h50m, clearly labelled illustrative, not for navigation). A circular 24-hour dial around the moon marks tide highs/lows and the current time. Text: phase name, illumination %, days until the next full/new moon, and the next (illustrative) high tide. Interaction: a date scrubber travels ±30 days so you can watch the moon wax and wane; hovering the sea makes it sparkle; a calm night mode dims the UI. Typography: a light, elegant serif with small caps.
```
