# 极简奢华腕表页

```
Design a product page for "Maison Varenne — Calibre 01", a fictional minimalist luxury watch. Palette: ivory #f5f1ea, champagne #d8c3a5, charcoal #1c1c1c and a hint of midnight blue #1b2a41 for a dial variant. The hero is a large, photoreal-feeling SVG watch built entirely from gradients: a brushed-steel case with conical highlights, a domed sapphire reflection, applied indices with tiny shadows, dauphine hands showing the REAL current local time, and a smoothly sweeping seconds hand (8 beats per second). Typography: a refined high-contrast serif for headings with wide-tracked small sans captions. Sections with generous whitespace: an exploded view of the movement whose layers separate on scroll; a dial variant selector (ivory / midnight / salmon) that recolours the watch with a smooth transition; a specification table; and a "Reserve" CTA. Interaction: hovering the watch tilts it slightly and moves the sapphire reflection; a "Night" toggle shows lume glowing on the hands and indices. Quiet, precise animation — nothing gaudy.
```
